CanvasX - Delete Your Data

CanvasX lets you delete individual canvases and their data without deleting your account, or you can delete your entire account and all associated data.

How to Delete a Canvas (Partial Data Deletion)

  1. Open the CanvasX app on your Android device.
  2. On the home screen, find the canvas you want to delete.
  3. Long-press the canvas or tap its menu icon.
  4. Select Delete Canvas.
  5. Confirm the deletion when prompted.

What Gets Deleted with a Canvas

  • The canvas record and all its metadata from Cloud Firestore.
  • All layer data, including images stored in Firebase Storage.
  • Realtime Database data associated with the canvas.
  • Local cache and thumbnails for the canvas on your device.

How to Delete Your Account (Full Data Deletion)

  1. Open the CanvasX app on your Android device.
  2. Tap the menu (three-dot icon) in the top-right corner.
  3. Select Delete Account.
  4. Confirm the deletion when prompted.

What Gets Deleted

  • Your Firebase Authentication account is permanently deleted.
  • Canvases you own without collaborators are deleted, including all canvas records, realtime data, and stored image files.
  • Canvases you own with collaborators are transferred to another collaborator. Your membership and library entry are removed.
  • Canvases where you are a collaborator — your membership and library entry are removed. The canvas remains available to its owner.
  • Your Firestore user document, if present, is deleted.
  • Realtime Database operation logs tied to your account are deleted from shared canvases.
  • Your local canvas cache is cleared when the signed-in account changes.

What Is Not Deleted

  • Images you previously exported to your device gallery or shared to other apps.
  • Shared canvas content that remains available to remaining collaborators.
  • Service logs or records that Google/Firebase may retain under their own policies and legal obligations.
Note: Account deletion is permanent and cannot be undone. Make sure to export any important canvases before deleting your account.
